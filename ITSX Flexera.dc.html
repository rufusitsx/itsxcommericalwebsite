Technology Value Assessment

One assessment. One clear decision.

The ITSX + Flexera Technology Value Assessment is a focused 30–45-day process designed to turn your existing technology data into an evidence-backed executive decision.

Using Flexera technology intelligence and your own data, ITSX focuses the assessment on two to four areas where the greatest potential value or risk exists.

Technology visibility and data qualityLifecycle and end-of-support exposurePublisher and application concentrationSoftware estate optimisationCloud spend and wasteCloud rate optimisationCMDB enrichment

Before we start

Three things need to be in place.

1

A material business question

There must be a real outcome worth investigating.

We agree the specific questions that matter, why they matter and what value answering them could create.

2

The right stakeholders

An executive sponsor and the relevant technical stakeholders need to be engaged.

The evidence must reach the people capable of making and supporting the decision.

3

A clear decision process

Before the assessment begins, we agree how the evidence will be considered when the assessment concludes.

This keeps the work focused on situations where the assessment can lead to a meaningful decision. It is also why assessment capacity is intentionally limited.

What you receive

The assessment produces six clear outputs designed to support action.

Executive summary

2

Technology baseline

3

Validated evidence

The agreed use cases, results, success criteria and supporting technical evidence.

12-month action plan

Priorities, owners, dependencies, estimated impact and the next decisions required.

5

Three-year roadmap

A longer-term path covering the relevant technology capabilities, services and governance.

Commercial options

Clear options showing what each potential path would involve, enabling an informed decision at the readout.

Who it’s for

Is your estate a good fit?

The assessment is designed for organisations with:

  • ✓A large or complex technology estate
  • ✓Significant software or cloud expenditure
  • ✓Relevant existing technology data

  • ✓Executive sponsorship
  • ✓A genuine decision to make
